    What can OCTAVIA reach?

    All optional. Keys stay on this PC.

    Discord: text and call it from your phone
    1. Open discord.com/developers/applications, "New Application" (any name).
    2. Bot: "Reset Token" and copy it. Turn on "Message Content Intent".
    3. OAuth2, URL Generator: tick "bot", then Send Messages, Read Message History, Attach Files, Connect and Speak. Open the link and add the bot to a server of yours.
    4. Discord settings, Advanced: Developer Mode on; right-click your name, "Copy User ID".

    It answers only you: anyone else is ignored, and on a call only your voice is heard.
