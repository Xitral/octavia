    All optional. Keys stay on this PC.

    Discord text and call it from your phone
    1. At discord.com/developers/applications, make a New Application.
    2. Under Bot, reset and copy the token, and turn on Message Content Intent.
    3. Under OAuth2, URL Generator, tick "bot" plus Send Messages, Read Message History, Attach Files, Connect and Speak. Open the link and add the bot to your server.
    4. In Discord's settings, turn on Developer Mode, then right-click your name and Copy User ID.

    It only answers you.
